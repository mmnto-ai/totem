A thread with more comments than the ten-comment window is judged on what was read: evidence found inside the window or at PR level discharges it, and a window with none is a bare resolve whose reason names the window — a fact-side deny at every tier, never the unevaluable class. Provenance carries `dischargedHigh` and `dischargedBy` (in-thread reply vs PR-level disposition) beside `highInline`, which now counts what still applies after the discharge, and a discharge prints one audit line on stderr naming the count and the arms.
