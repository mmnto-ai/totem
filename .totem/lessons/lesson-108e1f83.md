## Lesson — A prose-only row splits its recorded rules three ways

**Tags:** whitelist, authoring-ledger, cert-corpus, re-pin, measurement, spine
**Scope:** packages/cli/src/authored-rule-intake.ts, packages/cli/src/commands/authored-whitelist.ts, packages/cli/src/commands/spine-authored-cert-corpus.ts

Tagging the exemplar row for the forbidden-literal-token regex class prose-only (mmnto-ai/totem#2996) does three different things to a rule already recorded under it, and the build spec's first sentence, that every such rule re-derives `revised`, was false (the first falsification leg's one MATERIAL finding). A rule whose globs are all prose re-derives `revised`, because the recorded conjunct enters the ledger material, and `verifyOnly` refuses it until a re-pin. A rule with any non-prose glob, source-only or mixed, is refused by the intake in `runRuleAuthor`: it lands in the rejected list with a reason derived from the recorded cause (the failing glob and the four extensions, never the pair-mismatch sentence for a pair that matched), mints nothing and appends no row, and a re-pin cannot admit it. And the cert corpus refuses the whole envelope while it carries either kind: `buildAuthoredCertifyingCorpus` re-derives with `verifyOnly`, which throws when any entry would be authored, and then throws on a non-empty rejected list. Measured at the merge head on scratch copies: nine rules under the row (three prose-only, four source-only, two mixed), and the committed ten-entry envelope read unchanged 7, revised 1, rejected 2. The counts had to be taken one entry at a time, each under its own ledger row's `judgedBy`, because a whole-envelope `verifyOnly` re-derive throws and returns no count. The bring-forward leg confirmed the method by running the envelope without the revised entry and getting the same 7 and 2. So the committed envelope does not certify at this version until the owner-form re-pin replaces it, a state the round disclosed and a review bot raised as its one finding. The re-pin is tracked on mmnto-ai/totem#2988 and is read and written at this version or later.
