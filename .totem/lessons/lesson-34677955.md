## Lesson — Prune branches instead of aborting sweeps

**Tags:** traversal, performance
**Scope:** packages/**/*.ts, !**/*.test.*, !**/*.spec.*

A bound added to a recursive filesystem sweep decides abort-versus-prune per bound. A breadth bound (too many directories visited) aborts the whole sweep, because nothing after it is honest; a depth bound prunes only the branch it hit, and records a cut only when something lies below the pruned directory — an empty leaf at the bound leaves the sweep complete. Sharing one truncated flag with an early return at the top of the walk made the first deep branch end the sweep before a sibling stray at depth 1 was seen, and recording every depth-bound leaf as a cut made the doctor row warn on a complete sweep of this repository. The test for a bound plants a sibling after the pruned branch and an empty leaf at the bound (the Stray Markers row, mmnto-ai/totem#2974, legs 2 and 3).
