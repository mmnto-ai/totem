## Lesson — A whitelist class name promises its matcher's exact shape

**Tags:** whitelist, ast-grep, structural-class, documentation, gate5
**Scope:** packages/cli/src/commands/authored-whitelist.ts

A `(engine, structuralClass)` row names a decidable shape, and the words beside it must state that shape no wider than the matcher. The batch-4 rows (mmnto-ai/totem#3020) carry the breadth the replayer's probes measured: `forbidden-object-member-call` is a dot-member call, so `console.info('x')` matches and a computed access `console['warn']('x')` does not; `named-callee-argument-shape` sees an object-literal second argument with a constant or a variable first argument and misses a variable options argument, where the defect's prose "so the arguments array is absent" is not what the class decides; `try-block-expect-fail-with-catch` requires the call in the TRY block, after at least one statement and directly in the block, with a catch that binds a name, so a nested call, the call as the block's first statement and a catch without a binding do not match. The falsification leg's second finding was exactly a dropped qualifier: the changeset wrote "in the try block", and the published CHANGELOG would have read broader than the class until "directly in the try block" was folded in (deposit `922ec45c…`, F2). The name promises the shape, not a detection of tests that pass by accident; a reader who widens it widens what the intake admits.
