## Lesson — Expose all comparison inputs for verification

**Tags:** workflow, github-cli, developer-experience
**Scope:** packages/cli/src/commands/**/*.ts, !**/*.test.*, !**/*.spec.*

Skills that require manual verification steps must provide explicit, non-interactive commands to retrieve all necessary comparison inputs. Omitting key inputs, such as the current head SHA or summary comment SHAs, forces operators to improvise and defeats the deterministic quality of the check.
