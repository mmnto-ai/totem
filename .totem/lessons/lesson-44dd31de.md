## Lesson — Cite a ruling as of the commit that records it when its record keeps a slot open

**Tags:** manual, provenance, rulings, legs

When the record of a ruling keeps a re-ruling slot open, a citation says 'as recorded at' the commit and states no later state; check that no pair or verdict moves under any option of the open question, and carry the open slot to the counterpart who owns it (mmnto-ai/totem#2984, leg finding b3r-F3).
