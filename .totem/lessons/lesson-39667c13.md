## Lesson — A keep-out account names each rule's own ground

**Tags:** manual, migration, provenance, legs

When a table types several rules under one gate, the ruled reading is the ground only for the rules that turn on it; rules that fail under either reading, and pairs carried from an earlier batch on that batch's census, are said so, per rule, from the table's own rows (mmnto-ai/totem#2984, leg finding b3r-F1: six engine-typing keep-outs, of which two turn on the modal reading, two fail under either reading and two are carried).
