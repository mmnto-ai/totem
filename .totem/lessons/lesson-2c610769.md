## Lesson — Three kinds of rows share one word in the Gate 5 comments

**Tags:** whitelist, gate5, documentation, terminology
**Scope:** packages/cli/src/commands/authored-whitelist.ts

In the Gate 5 vocabulary "rows" names three different things: the whitelist's `(engine, structuralClass)` rows in `AUTHORED_WHITELIST`, the authoring-ledger rows the intake appends to `.totem/spine/authoring-ledger.ndjson`, and the scorer's per-entry rows of record (the C0–C7 composite and the admission column). The batch-4 table comment first said the five kept-out rules are "never rows" (whitelist rows) and then, of `b3e3e2b3`, that "its rows of record are the replayer's" (the scorer's rows under D3 (ii)), so a reader could take that rule to have whitelist rows owned by the replayer. The fact was right (the delivery mail's keep-out table and the scorer's batch-4 class-review table on strategy main, in its JSON form, say the entry's C0–C7 and admission rows of record are the replayer's); the sentence was folded to "its C0–C7 and admission rows of record are the replayer's" (mmnto-ai/totem#3020, deposit `922ec45c…`, F1). When a comment in this file uses "rows" for anything but whitelist rows, it names which kind.
