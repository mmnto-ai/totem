## Lesson — A changeset states the gate a diff moves, not the outcome downstream of it

**Tags:** manual, changesets, intake, legs

Passing the whitelist predicate is the first gate of the intake, not its result: the rest of the intake still runs, an existing identity reads unchanged or revised rather than minted, and exit 1 is the run's signal when any rule is rejected, not a per-entry outcome. A changeset that says 'decidable (minted)' claims more than a data row delivers (mmnto-ai/totem#2984, leg finding b3r-F2).
