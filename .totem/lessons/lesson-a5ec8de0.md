## Lesson — measure the class a styleguide finding targets, never the file-wide ratio

**Tags:** review-guidance, gca, spike, error-handling, styleguide, measurement

**Applies-to:** infrastructure

A styleguide finding on a throw's spelling is folded or declined on the convention of the finding's CLASS in that module, never on a file-wide ratio. On mmnto-ai/totem#3095 (2026-10-16) Gemini Code Assist asked that the new corpus-parse throws under spikes/spine-adopt/src wrap JSON.parse in a try/catch and carry the `[Totem Error]` prefix (styleguide § 3). A first count said 2 of the harness's 100 throw sites carried the prefix and drafted a decline with a § 6 styleguide entry; that count was wrong twice over and the decline never committed. A same-line grep for the throw and the prefix misses multi-line throws (five sites carry it, not two), and the K8 control's regex in controls.mts parses the SHIPPED parser's `[Totem Error] <file>: <keyPath> — <detail>` form out of a rejection message, not the harness's own throws, so it is no reason either way. The class measurement decides: every pre-existing site in the harness that catches a failed parse of a committed artifact and rethrows carries the prefix and the cause (controls.mts's artifact reader, t5-sweep.mts's lowering index, certify-verify.mts's committed manifest — three of three), so the bot's spelling IS the harness's convention for that throw, and the finding was folded whole as `parseCorpus` in spike-env.mts. Rule: before declining a styleguide spelling as "not this module's convention", enumerate the sites of the same class (here a catch-and-rethrow of a committed artifact's parse) with a multi-line-aware read and let that count decide; a file-wide ratio over unrelated throw classes proves nothing about the one in hand, and a measurement taken to support a decline already drafted is the one to re-take.
