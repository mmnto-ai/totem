## Lesson — Read what the harness mocks before adding a suggested test; pin an ordering with a throwing seam

**Tags:** manual, testing, legs

Before adding a test a reviewer or leg named, read what the harness mocks on that path: when the mocked seam swallows the behaviour, the suggested test passes on both sides and proves nothing; pin the ordering with a seam that throws or records, and RED it against the specific state it is meant to distinguish (leg 3 on mmnto-ai/totem#2965).
