## Lesson — The add_lesson sync reports how it ended, not a boolean

**Tags:** mcp, process, error-handling
**Scope:** packages/mcp/src/tools/**/*.ts, !**/*.test.*

The MCP `add_lesson` tool runs an incremental sync after it writes the lesson file, and that run ends one of four ways: `ok`, `timed-out` (killed at the 60 s cap), `failed` (a non-zero exit) or `spawn-error` (the process never started). When the vector store could not be opened and the sync is the rebuilder, the reply is chosen by that outcome, because the cure differs: a finished sync rebuilt the store, a timed-out one is resumed by the next sync, a failed one may have begun a rebuild and wants a full sync, and one that never started wants a full sync too. A success boolean could not tell the last three apart (mmnto-ai/totem#3012).
